Sand Boxes

Three course projects on the same puzzle: a tower of boxes filled with sand, spring-loaded gates, and the question of how to empty it at the lowest cost. Pick a project.

Assignment 1

Search algorithms on the gravity puzzle

The environment, the greedy approach from class, the four uninformed searches and the informed ones, compared on every one of the 59,049 initial states.

  • Animated simulator: open gates by hand or watch a plan
  • BFS, DFS, IDS, UCS, A*, RBFS, IDA*, Weighted A*
  • Histogram of Greedy_opt_ratio, scatter plots, time and memory
  • Proposed variant with water and concrete

Heuristic functions for informed search
